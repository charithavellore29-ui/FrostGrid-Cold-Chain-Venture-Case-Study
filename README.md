
# FrostGrid — Cold-Chain Venture Case Study

## Project Overview
FrostGrid is a proposed cold-chain logistics venture concept designed to improve temperature monitoring and reliability during the transportation of temperature-sensitive goods.

## Project Concept
The proposed concept explores combining:
- Blue Star refrigeration technology
- Delhivery logistics and transportation capabilities
- IoT-enabled temperature monitoring sensors
- A proposed FrostGrid Temperature Guarantee

## Flagship Offering
### FrostGrid Temperature Guarantee

A proposed temperature-assurance service that aims to monitor shipment temperature conditions throughout the cold-chain journey using IoT-enabled sensors and suitable refrigeration and logistics infrastructure.

## Business Focus
- Cold-chain logistics
- Temperature-sensitive supply chains
- IoT-based monitoring
- Logistics and infrastructure partnerships
- Business model development
- Strategic partnership analysis

## Project Type
Proposed Venture / Business Strategy Case Study

**Note:** FrostGrid is an academic venture concept, not an operating company or an executed merger between Blue Star and Delhivery.
